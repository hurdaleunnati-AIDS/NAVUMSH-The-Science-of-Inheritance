01 / YOUR GENETICS DICTIONARY

Understand the language of inheritance.

Gene: A region of DNA that contributes to a functional product or biological trait.

Allele: One of the alternative versions of a gene.

Genotype: The genetic combination being considered.

Phenotype: An observable characteristic influenced by genotype and often the environment.

Homozygous: Having two identical alleles at a gene location.

Heterozygous: Having two different alleles at a gene location.
