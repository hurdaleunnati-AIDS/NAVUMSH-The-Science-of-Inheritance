THE BLUEPRINT OF LIFE

01 / THE BEGINNING

Life carries a code.
Science helps us read it.

Every living organism carries genetic information. This information helps guide development, influences biological characteristics and passes from one generation to the next.

02 / THE FOUNDATIONS

Four ideas. One remarkable system.

01

DNA

Deoxyribonucleic acid stores the genetic instructions used in the growth, development and functioning of living organisms.

02

Genes

Genes are functional units of DNA that contribute to inherited biological information.

03

Chromosomes

Chromosomes are organised structures of DNA and proteins that carry genetic information.

04

Inheritance

Inheritance is the transmission of genetic information from parents to offspring.
